## ⚡ About The Project

**ETH BD Telegram Member Scraper** is a powerful Python-based tool that scrapes all members from any Telegram channel or group and automatically adds them to your own channel. Designed with a **Matrix-style hacker UI**, real-time progress tracking, and session persistence.

> 🎯 **Purpose:** Educational & Authorized Testing Only

### 🚀 Why This Tool?

- ✅ **One-time login** – Session saved forever
- ✅ **Matrix-style animated UI** – Hacker aesthetic
- ✅ **Real-time progress** – Speed & percentage
- ✅ **Auto-filter** – Bots & deleted accounts skipped
- ✅ **Smart delay** – Avoid rate limits
- ✅ **Cross-platform** – Windows, Linux, Android (Termux)
